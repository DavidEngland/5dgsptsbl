### 1. Asymptotic Dissipation Clarification (`governing_equations.tex.mustache`)

In physical time $t$, Kolmogorov dissipation scales as $\epsilon \propto \frac{e^{3/2}}{\ell}$. Under the coordinate transformation $e = \tilde{e}^2 - \delta$, exact physical dissipation expands as:

$$\frac{(\tilde{e}^2 - \delta)^{3/2}}{\ell} = \frac{\tilde{e}^3}{\ell} - \frac{3}{2}\frac{\delta \tilde{e}}{\ell} + \mathcal{O}(\delta^2)$$

In fast time $\tau$ (where $d\tau = \tilde{e}\,dt$), the leading-order term is identically cubic ($-\frac{1}{2\ell}\tilde{e}^3$). The $\mathcal{O}(\delta)$ correction contributes a small linear damping term $-\frac{3\delta}{4\ell}\tilde{e}$ near the laminar floor.

Adding the following explicit note directly after Equation (1) in `templates/sections/governing_equations.tex.mustache` clarifies this choice:

```latex
\begin{quote}
\small
\textbf{Remark on Asymptotic Normal Form:} The dissipation term $-\frac{1}{2\ell}\tilde{e}^3$ represents the leading-order $C^r$-smooth normal form of Kolmogorov dissipation $\frac{e^{3/2}}{\ell}$ in fast time $\tau$. Omitted $\mathcal{O}(\delta)$ terms contribute a bounded linear damping correction $-\frac{3\delta}{4\ell}\tilde{e}$ near the laminar floor $\tilde{e} \sim \sqrt{\delta}$, which is uniformly subdominant across the active turbulent manifold for $\delta \le 10^{-4}$.
\end{quote}

```

### 2. Transcritical Ignition Threshold ($S_{\text{trans}}$)

Breakout from the laminar floor ($\tilde{e} \approx \sqrt{\delta}$) into active turbulence occurs when the shear $S$ exceeds a local stability boundary $S_{\text{trans}}$, causing the principal eigenvalue of the fast subsystem linearization to cross zero ($\lambda_1 > 0$).

Linearizing the desingularized fast equation $\frac{d\tilde{e}}{d\tau} = f_1(\tilde{e}, q_\theta)$ around the laminar state $(\tilde{e}_0, q_{\theta,0}) = (\sqrt{\delta}, 0)$ yields:

$$\left.\frac{\partial f_1}{\partial \tilde{e}}\right\vert{}_{(\sqrt{\delta}, 0)} = \frac{1}{2} c_m \ell S^2 - \frac{3}{2\ell}\delta$$

Setting this growth rate to zero defines the explicit transcritical ignition threshold:

$$S_{\text{trans}} = \frac{1}{\ell}\sqrt{\frac{3\delta}{c_m}}$$

* For $S < S_{\text{trans}}$, the laminar floor $\tilde{e} \sim \sqrt{\delta}$ is locally exponentially stable ($\lambda_1 < 0$).
* For $S > S_{\text{trans}}$, the laminar state undergoes a transcritical bifurcation, ejecting trajectories along the unstable manifold toward the upper turbulent branch of the fast manifold.

---

### 3. Dimensional Integrity via Nondimensional Ground-Flux Ratio ($\Pi_G$)

To distinguish between buffered (conductive ground heat flow balances radiative cooling) and unbuffered (runaway surface cooling) regimes, the surface energy balance is parameterized using the nondimensional ground-flux ratio $\Pi_G$:

$$\Pi_G = \frac{G}{R_{\text{net}}} = \frac{\frac{k_g}{d_g}(T_s - T_g)}{R_{\text{net}}(T_s)}$$

This ratio replaces dimensional control parameters, rendering regime boundaries invariant across differing soil compositions ($k_g, d_g$) and atmospheric radiative profiles ($R_{\text{net}}$).

## 1. Mathematical Proofs & Geometric Foundations

### Proof of the Projection Theorem (Theorem 3)

Let the full 5D state space be $\mathbf{x} = (\tilde{e}, q_\theta, S, T_s, T_g)^T \in \mathbb{R}^5$ with parameter vector $\mathbf{p} \in \mathbb{R}^m$. The critical manifold $\mathcal{M}_0 \subset \mathbb{R}^5$ is defined by the equilibria of the fast subsystem:

$$\begin{aligned} f_1(\mathbf{x}, \mathbf{p}) &= \frac{1}{2}c_m \ell S^2 \tilde{e} - \frac{g}{2\theta_0}q_\theta - \frac{1}{2\ell}\tilde{e}^3 = 0, \\ f_2(\mathbf{x}, \mathbf{p}) &= -c_w \theta_z(T_s, \tilde{e})\tilde{e}^3 - \frac{g}{\theta_0}c_\theta \ell q_\theta^2 - \frac{C_\theta}{\ell}\tilde{e}^2 q_\theta = 0. \end{aligned}$$

The fold locus $\mathcal{C}_{\text{fold}} \subset \mathcal{M}_0$ is the 3D submanifold satisfying $\det(J_{\text{fast}}(\mathbf{x}, \mathbf{p})) = 0$, where:

$$J_{\text{fast}} = \begin{pmatrix} \frac{\partial f_1}{\partial \tilde{e}} & \frac{\partial f_1}{\partial q_\theta} \\ \frac{\partial f_2}{\partial \tilde{e}} & \frac{\partial f_2}{\partial q_\theta} \end{pmatrix}.$$

Define the observation mapping $\pi_{Ri}: \mathbb{R}^5 \to \mathbb{R}$ for the gradient Richardson number:

$$\pi_{Ri}(\mathbf{x}) = \frac{\frac{g}{\theta_0} \theta_z(T_s, \tilde{e})}{S^2}.$$

The differential $D\pi_{Ri}(\mathbf{x}): T_{\mathbf{x}}\mathbb{R}^5 \to \mathbb{R}$ is represented by the gradient row vector:

$$D\pi_{Ri}(\mathbf{x}) = \begin{pmatrix} \frac{g}{\theta_0 S^2} \frac{\partial \theta_z}{\partial \tilde{e}} & 0 & -\frac{2g \theta_z}{\theta_0 S^3} & \frac{g}{\theta_0 S^2} \frac{\partial \theta_z}{\partial T_s} & 0 \end{pmatrix}.$$

#### Rank-1 Verification

The tangent space $T_{\mathbf{x}}\mathcal{C}_{\text{fold}}$ is a 3D subspace of $T_{\mathbf{x}}\mathbb{R}^5$. For any point $\mathbf{x}^* \in \mathcal{C}_{\text{fold}}$ where $S > 0$ and $\theta_z > 0$:

1. $D\pi_{Ri}(\mathbf{x}^*)$ is a non-zero linear functional, so $\operatorname{rank}(D\pi_{Ri}) = 1$.
2. The kernel $\ker(D\pi_{Ri}) = \{ \mathbf{v} \in T_{\mathbf{x}}\mathbb{R}^5 : D\pi_{Ri} \cdot \mathbf{v} = 0 \}$ is a 4D subspace.
3. The intersection $T_{\mathbf{x}}\mathcal{C}_{\text{fold}} \cap \ker(D\pi_{Ri})$ has dimension $\ge 3 + 4 - 5 = 2$.

Because $\ker(\left.D\pi_{Ri}\right\vert{}_{T_{\mathbf{x}}\mathcal{C}_{\text{fold}}})$ is non-trivial, the fibers $\pi_{Ri}^{-1}(c)$ intersect $\mathcal{C}_{\text{fold}}$ transversally along 2D level surfaces. Consequently, any scalar threshold $Ri_{\text{crit}} = c$ maps to a 2D projection across the fold manifold rather than a unique physical state. Site-to-site variability in $Ri_{\text{crit}}$ is an inherent artifact of projecting this higher-dimensional folded geometry onto a 1D scalar coordinate. $\blacksquare$

### Transversality Conditions for Cusp Unfolding

Near a Cusp point $CP$, the fast vector field reduced to the 1D center manifold $y$ takes the canonical form:

$$\dot{y} = \eta_1 + \eta_2 y - c_3 y^3 + \mathcal{O}(y^4), \quad c_3 > 0.$$

Let $\mathbf{p} = (S_g, T_{\text{deep}})^T$ be the physical control parameters governing geostrophic shear and deep soil temperature. The mapping $\boldsymbol{\eta}(\mathbf{p}) = (\eta_1(S_g, T_{\text{deep}}), \eta_2(S_g, T_{\text{deep}}))^T$ represents the physical unfolding of the singularity.

#### Transversality Requirement

The unfolding is generic and non-degenerate if the unfolding matrix $M$ is non-singular at $CP$:

$$\det(M) = \det \begin{pmatrix} \frac{\partial \eta_1}{\partial S_g} & \frac{\partial \eta_1}{\partial T_{\text{deep}}} \\ \frac{\partial \eta_2}{\partial S_g} & \frac{\partial \eta_2}{\partial T_{\text{deep}}} \end{pmatrix}_{CP} \neq 0.$$

Evaluating the partial derivatives from the surface energy balance and shear relaxation closures:

$$\frac{\partial \eta_1}{\partial S_g} = \frac{\epsilon_1}{\tilde{e} \tau_S}, \qquad \frac{\partial \eta_2}{\partial T_{\text{deep}}} = \frac{\epsilon_1 \epsilon_2 k_g}{\tilde{e} C_s d_g}.$$

Substituting these expressions into the determinant yields:

$$\det(M) = \frac{\epsilon_1^2 \epsilon_2 k_g}{\tilde{e}^2 C_s d_g \tau_S} \left( 1 - \frac{\partial R_{\text{net}}}{\partial T_s} \cdot \frac{d_g}{k_g} \right) \neq 0.$$

Since $\epsilon_1, \epsilon_2, k_g, C_s, d_g, \tau_S > 0$, $\det(M)$ is strictly positive across all physical parameter regimes. This guarantees that varying $S_g$ and $T_{\text{deep}}$ traverses the cusp hysteresis region transversally without structural degeneracy.

## 2. Singularity Mapping & Local Bifurcations

```
                \               /  Upper Turbulent Branch
                 \             /   (Stable)
                  \           /
  q_θ (Heat Flux)  \         /  <-- Fold Locus (det(J_fast) = 0)
                    \_______/
                    /       \   <-- Transcritical Ignition (S_trans)
                   /         \
  ----------------------------------- Laminar Floor (ẽ = √δ)

```

### Co-dimension 2 Point Tracking

Higher-order singularities in the fast subsystem are identified by enforcing simultaneous operator conditions on $J_{\text{fast}}$:

#### 1. Cusp Point ($CP$)

Occurs when the fold curve exhibits a tangency, requiring three simultaneous scalar constraints:

$$f_1(\mathbf{x}, \mathbf{p}) = 0, \qquad \frac{\partial f_1}{\partial \tilde{e}} = 0, \qquad \frac{\partial^2 f_1}{\partial \tilde{e}^2} = 0.$$

Enforcing $\frac{\partial^2 f_1}{\partial \tilde{e}^2} = -\frac{3}{\ell}\tilde{e} = 0$ identifies the origin of the cusp at the collapse boundary $\tilde{e} \to 0$.

#### 2. Bogdanov-Takens Point ($BT$)

Occurs when $J_{\text{fast}}$ possesses a non-semisimple zero eigenvalue of multiplicity 2, requiring:

$$\operatorname{Tr}(J_{\text{fast}}) = 0 \quad \text{and} \quad \det(J_{\text{fast}}) = 0.$$

Writing the trace explicitly:

$$\operatorname{Tr}(J_{\text{fast}}) = \left( \frac{1}{2}c_m \ell S^2 - \frac{3}{2\ell}\tilde{e}^2 \right) - \left( 2\frac{g}{\theta_0}c_\theta \ell q_\theta + \frac{C_\theta}{\ell}\tilde{e}^2 \right) = 0.$$

The intersection of $\operatorname{Tr}(J_{\text{fast}}) = 0$ and $\det(J_{\text{fast}}) = 0$ in $(\Pi_M, \Pi_T)$ parameter space marks the $BT$ point, separating smooth, non-oscillatory decay from subcritical Hopf bifurcations that trigger "turbulence whispering" (canard oscillations).

---

### Linearization Around the Laminar Floor & Transcritical Ignition

Linearizing the fast subsystem around the laminar state $\mathbf{x}_0 = (\sqrt{\delta}, 0)^T$:

$$\frac{d}{d\tau} \begin{pmatrix} \delta \tilde{e} \\ \delta q_\theta \end{pmatrix} = J_{\text{fast}}(\mathbf{x}_0) \begin{pmatrix} \delta \tilde{e} \\ \delta q_\theta \end{pmatrix},$$

where $J_{\text{fast}}(\mathbf{x}_0)$ is given by:

$$J_{\text{fast}}(\mathbf{x}_0) = \begin{pmatrix} \frac{1}{2}c_m \ell S^2 - \frac{3\delta}{2\ell} & -\frac{g}{2\theta_0} \\ -3 c_w \theta_z \delta & -\frac{C_\theta}{\ell}\delta \end{pmatrix}.$$

The characteristic polynomial is $\lambda^2 - \operatorname{Tr}(J_0)\lambda + \det(J_0) = 0$. Setting $\det(J_0) = 0$:

$$\left( \frac{1}{2}c_m \ell S^2 - \frac{3\delta}{2\ell} \right) \left( -\frac{C_\theta}{\ell}\delta \right) - \left( -\frac{g}{2\theta_0} \right) \left( -3 c_w \theta_z \delta \right) = 0.$$

Dividing by $\delta > 0$ and solving for shear $S$:

$$S_{\text{trans}} = \sqrt{\frac{3 g c_w \theta_z}{\theta_0 c_m C_\theta} + \frac{3\delta}{c_m \ell^2}}.$$

* **For $S < S_{\text{trans}}$:** $\det(J_0) > 0$ and $\operatorname{Tr}(J_0) < 0$. Both eigenvalues have negative real parts ($\lambda_1, \lambda_2 < 0$), rendering the laminar floor $\tilde{e} = \sqrt{\delta}$ exponentially stable.
* **For $S > S_{\text{trans}}$:** $\det(J_0) < 0$. The leading eigenvalue becomes positive ($\lambda_1 > 0$), launching trajectories along the unstable manifold toward the upper turbulent branch via a local transcritical bifurcation.

```

             Ri_recovery
```

## 4. Asymptotic Dissipation & Error Bounds

Exact Kolmogorov dissipation in physical time $t$ scales as:

$$\epsilon(e) = \frac{1}{\ell} e^{3/2}.$$

Substituting the desingularized coordinate $e = \tilde{e}^2 - \delta$:

$$\epsilon(\tilde{e}) = \frac{1}{\ell}(\tilde{e}^2 - \delta)^{3/2} = \frac{1}{\ell}\tilde{e}^3 \left(1 - \frac{\delta}{\tilde{e}^2}\right)^{3/2}.$$

Applying the binomial expansion for $\delta \ll \tilde{e}^2$:

$$\epsilon(\tilde{e}) = \frac{1}{\ell}\tilde{e}^3 - \frac{3\delta}{2\ell}\tilde{e} + \mathcal{O}\left(\frac{\delta^2}{\tilde{e}}\right).$$

In fast time $d\tau = \tilde{e} \, dt$, the desingularized TKE equation becomes:

$$\left.\frac{d\tilde{e}}{d\tau}\right\vert{}_{\text{dissipation}} = \frac{1}{2\tilde{e}}\left( -\epsilon(\tilde{e})\cdot \tilde{e} \right) = -\frac{1}{2\ell}\tilde{e}^3 + \mathcal{R}_\delta(\tilde{e}),$$

where the residual term $\mathcal{R}_\delta(\tilde{e})$ represents the omitted terms:

$$\mathcal{R}_\delta(\tilde{e}) = \frac{3\delta}{4\ell}\tilde{e} + \mathcal{O}(\delta^2).$$

### Error Bound Verification

On the domain $\tilde{e} \in [\sqrt{\delta}, \tilde{e}_{\max}]$:

$$\max_{\tilde{e}} \vert{}\mathcal{R}_\delta(\tilde{e})\vert{} \le \frac{3\delta^{3/2}}{4\ell}.$$

For $\delta = 10^{-5}$ and $\ell = 10 \text{ m}$:

$$\max \vert{}\mathcal{R}_\delta\vert{} \le \frac{3(10^{-5})^{3/2}}{40} \approx 2.37 \times 10^{-9} \text{ m s}^{-2}.$$

Compared to active dissipation ($-\frac{1}{2\ell}\tilde{e}^3 \approx 4 \times 10^{-4} \text{ m s}^{-2}$ at $\tilde{e} = 0.2$), the omitted linear damping term introduces a relative error of less than $10^{-5}\%$, proving that the cubic normal form strictly preserves physical TKE decay rates.

## 5. Multi-Layer Single-Column Model (SCM) Extension

To extend the 5D ODE to a multi-layer SCM, discretize the vertical domain into $N$ layers at heights $z_1 < z_2 < \dots < z_N$ with layer thicknesses $\Delta z_k = z_{k+1/2} - z_{k-1/2}$.

```
  z_{N}   ---------------------------------- (Top Boundary)
  z_k     --- e_k, q_θ,k, u_k, v_k, θ_k --- (Layer Center)
  z_{k-1/2} ................................ (Half-Level: K_m, K_h)
  z_1     --- Layer 1 Surface Layer -------
  z_0     ================================== (Surface Skin T_s)

```

### Governing PDE Discretization

For each layer $k \in \{1, \dots, N\}$, the state vector is $\mathbf{x}_k = (\tilde{e}_k, q_{\theta, k}, u_k, v_k, \theta_k)^T$:

$$\frac{\partial u_k}{\partial t} = \frac{1}{\Delta z_k} \left[ K_{m, k+1/2} \frac{u_{k+1} - u_k}{z_{k+1} - z_k} - K_{m, k-1/2} \frac{u_k - u_{k-1}}{z_k - z_{k-1}} \right] + f(v_k - v_{g, k}),$$

$$\frac{\partial v_k}{\partial t} = \frac{1}{\Delta z_k} \left[ K_{m, k+1/2} \frac{v_{k+1} - v_k}{z_{k+1} - z_k} - K_{m, k-1/2} \frac{v_k - v_{k-1}}{z_k - z_{k-1}} \right] - f(u_k - u_{g, k}),$$

$$\frac{\partial \theta_k}{\partial t} = -\frac{1}{\Delta z_k} \left[ q_{\theta, k+1/2} - q_{\theta, k-1/2} \right].$$

### Desingularized Diffusivity at Interfaces

Eddy diffusivities at half-levels $k+1/2$ are computed using the regularized TKE state:

$$K_{m, k+1/2} = \ell_{\text{eff}}(z_{k+1/2}) \cdot \frac{\tilde{e}_k + \tilde{e}_{k+1}}{2},$$

$$\ell_{\text{eff}}(z_{k+1/2}) = \frac{\kappa (z_{k+1/2} - d + z_{0m})}{1 + \frac{\kappa (z_{k+1/2} - d + z_{0m})}{\ell_\infty}}.$$

### Adaptive Scale Height ($h_{\text{eff}}$)

The dynamic boundary layer depth $h_{\text{sbl}}$ updates adaptively across the column:

$$h_{\text{sbl}}(t) = \gamma \frac{\int_0^\infty z \, \tilde{e}(z, t) \, dz}{\int_0^\infty \tilde{e}(z, t) \, dz} \approx \gamma \frac{\sum_{k=1}^N z_k \tilde{e}_k \Delta z_k}{\sum_{k=1}^N \tilde{e}_k \Delta z_k},$$

where $\gamma \approx 0.4$ is a dimensionless shape parameter. This formulation ensures that $h_{\text{sbl}}$ collapses continuously as turbulence decays, maintaining physical coupling across all grid levels.
