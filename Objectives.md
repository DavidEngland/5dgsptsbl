The primary objective of the **5DGSPTSBL** project is to resolve the decades-old "Richardson Number Universality Crisis" in boundary-layer meteorology by formulating a mathematically rigorous 5D Geometric Singular Perturbation Theory (GSPT) framework for the Stable Boundary Layer (SBL).

---

### Core Project Objectives

* **Theoretical & Philosophical:** Prove the **Projection Theorem** (Theorem 3), demonstrating that observed critical Richardson numbers ($Ri_{\text{crit}}$) across disparate field campaigns (e.g., CASES-99 vs. SHEBA) are scalar 1D projections of a single, universal, higher-dimensional folded manifold deforming under varying surface energy balances, rather than evidence of conflicting physical laws.
* **Mathematical & Geometric Regularization:** Apply a $C^\infty$-smooth coordinate chart transformation ($\tilde{e} = \sqrt{e + \delta}$) to desingularize low-TKE states ($e \to 0$). This enables the application of Fenichel theory, fold locus tracking ($\det J_{\text{fast}} = 0$), and formal classification of nocturnal intermittency—identifying pre-burst "turbulence whispering" as deterministic oscillations within canard funnels near folded nodes, and re-ignition as a local transcritical bifurcation ($S > S_{\text{trans}}$).
* **Bifurcation & Regime Mapping:** Map co-dimension 2 singularities (Cusp and Bogdanov-Takens points) and measure hysteresis width ($\Delta Ri_H$) across dimensionless mechanical ($\Pi_M$), thermal ($\Pi_T$), and ground-flux ($\Pi_G$) similarity groups to rigorously delineate regimes of smooth continuous decay from those of abrupt nocturnal collapse.
* **Operational & Applied Meteorology:** Eliminate chronic operational defects in Numerical Weather Prediction (NWP) and climate models—such as "runaway surface cooling", loss of nocturnal inversions, and unphysical over-mixing—by replacing static diagnostic stability functions with dynamic manifold geometry, including turning-point $H_{\text{max}}$ sensible heat flux limiting, prognostic heat flux ($q_\theta$), and adaptive boundary layer scale heights ($h_{\text{sbl}}$).
* **Computational Software & Pipeline:** Provide an open-source, fully reproducible Julia research framework complete with automated Jacobian sensitivity suites, WSINDy-based campaign parameter calibration, single-column model (SCM) solvers, and a reproducible LaTeX paper generation pipeline.
